A LITTLE STORY BEHIND JENNY

Every little life deserves a second chance.

Jenny was found alone in an oil palm plantation in East Kalimantan. After spending around five months with a local resident, she was rescued and brought to Samboja Lestari on 3 January 2025, when she was just 18 months old.

At first, the new surroundings felt unfamiliar and frightening. With the care of her surrogate mothers, Jenny gradually began to adapt. About a month later, she was joining Forest School and showing her climbing skills. And she even had a cheeky moment trying to steal a friend's papaya. 🧡
